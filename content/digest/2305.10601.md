Tree of Thoughts generalises chain-of-thought prompting by letting a model explore several candidate reasoning steps at once instead of committing to a single left-to-right path. Prompts ask the model to propose thoughts, evaluate them with a scoring heuristic and search over the resulting tree using look-ahead and backtracking. On tasks that require planning, such as the game of twenty-four and creative writing puzzles, this deliberate search raises success rates dramatically over standard prompting, showing that the structure of the inference process itself is a powerful lever for reasoning quality.
