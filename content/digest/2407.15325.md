Odyssey presents an LLM-based agent framework for long-horizon exploration in the open world of Minecraft. The agent maintains a growing library of primitive and compositional skills, retrieves them by description when a new task arrives, and uses a LLaMA-3 model fine-tuned on Minecraft-wiki question answering to ground its decisions. The authors also release a capability benchmark that measures long-term planning, dynamic plan revision and autonomous exploration. Experiments show that the skill library and wiki knowledge substantially improve task completion in unfamiliar terrain compared with flat instruction-following baselines.
