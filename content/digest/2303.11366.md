Reflexion gives language agents an episodic memory of verbal self-reflection rather than weight updates. After an unsuccessful attempt the agent is asked to write a short critique describing what went wrong, and that critique is stored and replayed in later trials. In the paper this loop improves success rates on coding benchmarks, multi-step reasoning puzzles and interactive decision-making environments. Because learning happens entirely through language, the policy can be revised after only a handful of attempts and the same procedure transfers across tasks without task-specific fine-tuning.
